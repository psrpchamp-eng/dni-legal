Privacy Policy

DNI Bot · Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information DNI (“the Bot”) may collect or process, how it may be used, and how privacy requests can be made.

The bot’s exact database and retention configuration has not yet been confirmed. This policy uses cautious wording and must be checked against the deployed code and hosting setup before publication as a final policy.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on enabled features and server configuration, DNI may collect or process Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames and display names, moderation records (such as warnings, timeouts, and bans), server activity and event logs, ticket information and messages submitted through ticket features, giveaway participation information, welcome and leave event information, command usage and interaction information, and server configuration settings.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide bot features; execute moderation commands; maintain moderation and activity logs; manage support tickets and giveaways; deliver welcome and leave messages; respond to commands; maintain server settings; troubleshoot issues; prevent abuse; and improve reliability and performance.

3. Message and Moderation Logging

DNI supports logging features that may record server activities and moderation events. Depending on server configuration and Discord permissions, this may include message deletion or editing events, moderation actions, member joins and leaves, ticket activity, and other configured events. Server administrators are responsible for configuring logging appropriately and informing members about relevant logging practices.

4. Storage and Security

Information may be processed or stored through hosting infrastructure, configuration files, databases, or application logs, depending on the Bot’s implementation. The exact storage method and retention settings should be confirmed against the current deployment. Reasonable measures are intended to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction; however, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing

We do not sell users’ personal information. Information may be processed by third-party infrastructure or hosting providers when necessary to operate DNI, and may be disclosed when required by applicable law.

6. Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the operation of DNI, its features, security, or applicable legal requirements. Retention periods may differ by information type. The operator should publish specific retention periods once they have been verified and configured.

7. Privacy Requests

Depending on applicable privacy laws, users may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of eligible personal information. Requests may be submitted to the DNI development team through the support channels available in the Discord server where DNI is used. Requests are subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate retention requirements.

8. Children’s Privacy

DNI is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age permitted by applicable laws and Discord’s requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

DNI relies on Discord and may use external hosting or infrastructure providers. Those services have their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as DNI develops. Updates will be published with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the DNI development team through the support channels available in the Discord server where DNI is used.
